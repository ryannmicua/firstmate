## Live limitation

With HOME and XDG_CONFIG_HOME relocated inside the worktree, prepare reports `fleet-state tripwire requires exactly one running default session`. Creating or modifying a default session is expressly forbidden by the trusted lab contract. No Herdr lab was provisioned. The inactive-session non-autostart regression passed with a fake transport and is supplemental evidence only. Live validation requires permission for the guarded helper's scoped Herdr session files outside the worktree, or a pre-provided isolated session store satisfying its default-session tripwire.
